PRIMARY RESEARCH THESIS

Color is not merely a secondary attribute of a pre-existing form.
It can operate as an autonomous relational principle through
which visual structures, spatial relations, rhythm and temporal
perception emerge.

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATIVE DISTINCTION

The research does not claim that Sonia Delaunay anticipated
digital image generation or contemporary artificial intelligence.

The relationship between simultaneism and algorithmic visual
systems is discussed as a structural analogy, not as a claim
of historical or technological identity.
